Our Story

A neighborhood barrel, full of good coffee.

Coffee Barrel started with a simple idea: bring honest, carefully sourced coffee to East Pasadena. No shortcuts, no fuss — just beans roasted in small batches, ground to order, and brewed by people who care about the craft.

Tucked into a small storefront on Colorado Blvd, we've become the morning ritual for regulars from Hastings Ranch to San Marino. Pull up a stool, watch the pour-over bloom, and stay a while. The Wi-Fi is fast and the refills are endless.
