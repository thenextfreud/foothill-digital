Our Story

A little piece of Hawaii on East Colorado Blvd.

Ry grew up on the North Shore of Oahu, where poke isn't a trend — it's breakfast, lunch, and dinner. After moving to Pasadena, he missed the taste of freshly caught ahi cubed by hand, seasoned with shoyu and sesame, and served over warm rice.

So he opened Ry's Poke Shack. Every fish is sashimi-grade, every sauce is made in-house, and every bowl is built exactly how you want it. No freezers, no shortcuts — just the freshest catch and the aloha spirit that comes with it.
