Our Story

A family tile shop on Foothill Boulevard.

Tap N Tile has been serving Pasadena homeowners and contractors since 1998. What started as a small tile supply shop has grown into a full-service kitchen and bath contracting company — but the commitment to quality hasn't changed.

We handle everything from a single bathroom backsplash to a full kitchen remodel with custom countertops. Porcelain, ceramic, natural stone, glass mosaics — we know the material, we know the installation, and we stand behind every job.

What We Do

From substrate to seal, we handle it all.

Tile Installation

Floor, wall, and shower tile installation. Porcelain, ceramic, travertine, marble, and glass mosaics — set level, grouted clean, and sealed to last.

Countertop Fabrication

Granite, quartz, marble, and solid surface countertops. Custom templating, fabrication, and installation — seamless joints and polished edges.

Kitchen Remodels

Full kitchen renovations — cabinets, countertops, backsplashes, flooring, and plumbing fixtures. We manage the project from demo to final inspection.

Bathroom Remodels

Complete bathroom renovations — walk-in showers, tub surrounds, vanities, tile floors, and waterproofing. Built to code, sealed against moisture.

Plumbing Fixtures

Faucets, sinks, toilets, shower systems, and fixture upgrades. We carry and install major brands — Moen, Delta, Kohler, and more.

Design Consultation

Material selection, layout planning, and design guidance. We help you choose the right tile, stone, and fixtures for your space and budget.
