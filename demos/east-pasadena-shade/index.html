Our Story

A Pasadena tradition for nearly 60 years.

East Pasadena Shade Co has been crafting custom window treatments since 1965. For nearly six decades, we've helped Pasadena homeowners and businesses find the perfect draperies, blinds, shutters, and shades for their spaces.

We're a small, family-owned business that takes pride in personal service. We don't just sell you off-the-shelf products — we come to your home, measure your windows, help you choose the right style and fabric, and install everything to perfection.

What We Do

Custom window treatments, start to finish.

Free In-Home Consultation

We come to your home, measure your windows, and help you choose the perfect treatment. No obligation, no pressure — just expert advice.

Custom Draperies

Hand-sewn draperies in your choice of fabric, style, and lining. Pinch pleat, grommet, rod pocket, and more. Made to your exact measurements.

Blinds & Shades

Wood blinds, faux wood, mini blinds, roller shades, Roman shades, cellular shades, and motorized options. Light control and privacy in style.

Plantation Shutters

Custom plantation shutters in wood and composite. Timeless, elegant, and energy-efficient. Measured and installed by our team.

Professional Installation

We don't just sell you window treatments — we install them. Proper installation means they look great, operate smoothly, and last for years.

Motorization

Motorized blinds and shades for hard-to-reach windows and smart home integration. Control with a remote, app, or voice assistant.
