Meet Your Doctor

Dr. Vincent Wang, DDS, MS

Dr. Vincent Wang is a board-certified periodontist committed exclusively to the practice of periodontology and dental implants. He grew up in Rockville, Maryland and earned his Bachelor of Science from Johns Hopkins University and Master of Science from George Washington University.

As a Diplomate of the American Board of Periodontology, Dr. Wang meets the highest standards of education and training in his specialty. He also teaches at the UCLA School of Dentistry, sharing his expertise with the next generation of dentists.

Our Treatments

Advanced periodontal care, tailored to you.

LANAP Laser Surgery

Minimally invasive laser treatment for gum disease. No cutting, no stitches, faster recovery compared to traditional surgery.

Dental Implants

Permanent tooth replacement that looks and functions like natural teeth. Single implants to full-arch restoration.

Gingival Tissue Graft

Gum grafting to treat receding gums, reduce sensitivity, and improve the appearance of your smile.

3D CT Scanning

Advanced 3D imaging with significantly less radiation than traditional X-rays for precise diagnosis and treatment planning.

Digital Scanning

Comfortable digital impressions — no messy putty. Precise 3D models of your gums and teeth for accurate treatment.

Periodontal Surgery

Traditional surgical treatments for advanced gum disease when conservative approaches aren't sufficient.

Detailed Examination

Dr. Wang conducts a thorough examination lasting 40-60 minutes. He'll discuss your diagnosis, treatment options, timeline, and cost.
