Our Story

A salon on Foothill Boulevard.

Blue Diamond Salon is a full-service hair and nail salon in East Pasadena. We've been serving the community on Foothill Blvd, offering everything from a quick trim to a full makeover — hair, nails, color, and styling all under one roof.

Our experienced stylists work with all hair types and textures. Whether you need a classic cut, a bold new color, or a full set of nails for a special occasion, we'll make sure you leave looking and feeling your best.

What We Do

Hair, nails, and everything between.

Haircuts & Styling

Men's, women's, and children's haircuts. Blowouts, updos, and special occasion styling. We work with all hair types and textures.

Color & Highlights

Full color, highlights, balayage, ombre, and color correction. Premium products and experienced colorists for results you'll love.

Manicures

Classic, gel, and acrylic manicures. Nail art, French tips, and custom designs. Long-lasting, chip-resistant finishes.

Pedicures

Classic and spa pedicures with massage, exfoliation, and polish. Relax and pamper your feet in our comfortable pedicure area.

Special Occasions

Weddings, proms, parties, and events. Updos, makeup, and full styling packages. Group bookings available for bridal parties.

Treatments

Deep conditioning, keratin treatments, scalp treatments, and hair repair. Restore shine, strength, and health to your hair.
