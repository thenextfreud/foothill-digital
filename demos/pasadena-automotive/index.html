Our Story

Auto repair specialists on Foothill Boulevard.

Pasadena Automotive Repair is a full-service automotive repair company serving the Greater Pasadena Area. We specialize in the use of diagnostic technology — which means we don't guess, we know. When your check engine light comes on, we have the tools and expertise to find the real problem.

What We Do

Full-service repair, diagnostics, and alignment.

Engine Diagnostics

Advanced OBD-II diagnostic technology to pinpoint engine problems fast. Check engine light, misfires, emissions issues — we find the real cause, not just the symptom.

Alignment Services

Precision wheel alignment to keep your car driving straight and your tires wearing evenly. Computerized alignment for all vehicles, including trucks and SUVs.

Oil Changes

Conventional, synthetic blend, and full synthetic oil changes. Fast service with quality filters and the right grade for your engine.

Brake Service

Brake pad and rotor replacement, caliper service, brake fluid flush. Squeaking, grinding, or soft pedal — we'll diagnose and fix it.

A/C Service

Auto AC repair and recharge, including the new HFO-1234yf systems. Hybrid and electric vehicle A/C service available.

Maintenance & Tune-Ups

Scheduled maintenance, 30/60/90k services, spark plugs, belts, hoses, filters. Keep your car running like new and avoid costly repairs.
