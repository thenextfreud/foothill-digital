What We Do

Full auto glass service, mobile or in-shop.

Windshield Replacement

Full windshield replacement with OEM-quality glass. All makes and models. Proper adhesive cure time and safe-drive-away guarantee on every install.

Rock Chip Repair

Small chips and cracks up to 3 inches can often be repaired without replacing the whole windshield. Fast, affordable, and usually covered by insurance.

Side Window Replacement

Driver and passenger side window replacement. Tempered and laminated glass available for all vehicle types — cars, trucks, SUVs, and vans.

Rear Window Replacement

Back glass and rear windshield replacement, including defrost grid reconnection. Heated and privacy glass options for most vehicles.

Mobile Auto Glass

We come to you — home, office, or anywhere in the San Gabriel Valley. Mobile service at no extra charge for most locations within Pasadena and surrounding areas.

Insurance Claims

We work with all major insurance companies and handle the paperwork for you. Most comprehensive policies cover chip repair with zero deductible.
