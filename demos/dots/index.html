Our Story

From a cupcake shop to a neighborhood cafe.

Dots started in 2006 as a humble cupcake shop built to bring happiness and joy to Pasadena. Founder Hailey Kwon wanted to capture the spirit of that shop in a full cafe — a bright, cheerful space filled with love and fresh food for all to enjoy.

In 2018, that dream became reality when Dots Cafe & Bakery opened on East Colorado Blvd. Today, Hailey and her mom (Umma) bake from scratch every morning, serve farm-fresh breakfast and lunch, and make the signature cupcakes that started it all.

— Hailey & Umma
