Our Story

A family kitchen serving Pasadena's favorites for over 25 years.

M&K Chinese Food has been a Colorado Blvd staple since the late 1990s. What started as a small takeout counter has grown into the neighborhood's go-to for classic Chinese-American cooking — the kind of place where the wok is always hot and the egg rolls are always fresh.

Every dish is made to order. No heat lamps, no shortcuts. The owners still hand-roll the egg rolls every morning, mix the orange chicken sauce from scratch, and remember the regulars by name. It's the comfort food you grew up with, done right.
