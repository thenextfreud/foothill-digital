Our Story

A Colorado Blvd landmark for quality antiques.

Michelle Beiner has been sourcing and selling quality antiques, lighting, and decorative art in East Pasadena for decades. Her shop on Colorado Blvd is a destination for collectors, designers, and anyone looking for that perfect piece with a story behind it.

From Spanish Revival to Mid-Century Modern, Arts & Crafts to Art Deco — every piece is hand-selected for quality, character, and authenticity. Michelle also offers expert antique lighting re-wiring and restoration, and runs a successful estate sale company.
