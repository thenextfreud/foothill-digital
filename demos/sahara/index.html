Our Story

Three decades of Mediterranean flavor on Colorado Blvd.

Sahara Restaurant has been serving the East Pasadena community since 1992. For 33 years, the Krikorian family has been grilling kebabs over mesquite charcoal, hand-making falafel from garbanzo beans and fava beans, and baking fresh pita bread daily.

What started as a small family kitchen has become a Pasadena institution — the kind of place where the owner knows your name and the recipes haven't changed because they were already perfect.
