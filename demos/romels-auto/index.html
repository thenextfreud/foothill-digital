Our Story

A craftsman's shop on Foothill Boulevard.

Romel's Auto Upholstery has been restoring vehicle interiors in East Pasadena for years — one panel, one seam, one seat at a time. It's the kind of shop where the work speaks for itself, and where customers come back because the stitching holds up.

We work on everything from daily drivers to classic cars and custom builds. Premium leather grades, durable marine vinyl, OEM-matched fabrics — sourced right, cut by hand, and installed with the kind of attention you can't get from a mass-production shop.

What We Do

Full-service upholstery, inside and out.

Seat Reupholstery

Full leather and vinyl seat rebuilds — new foam, premium hides, and double-stitched seams. We match OEM patterns or build something custom.

Headliners

Sagging or stained headliner replacement. Premium suede, alcantara, or OEM-matched fabric — re-boarded and re-installed tight, no wrinkles.

Door Panels

Door panel restoration and custom upholstery. New armrest pads, map pockets, and inserts — stitched and fitted to factory specs or better.

Convertible Tops

Convertible top replacement and repair. Premium canvas or vinyl, new tension cables, and rear window installation — sealed and aligned right.

Custom Stitching

Contrast stitching, diamond quilting, embroidered logos, and two-tone layouts. Custom thread colors and stitch patterns to make it yours.

Boat & Motorcycle Seats

Marine-grade vinyl and UV-stable thread for boat seats and cushions. Motorcycle seat reshaping and re-covering for comfort and style on long rides.
